Wenling Zhou  |  周文玲

Welcome to my homepage!

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Mathematics at Shandong University, China. My research focuses on extremal graph theory and hypergraph theory, including Turán problems, matchings, and tilings.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Shandong University and Université Paris-Saclay in 2023, under the supervision of Guanghui Wang and Hao Li.

Research Interests: Extremal graph theory and hypergraph theory.
